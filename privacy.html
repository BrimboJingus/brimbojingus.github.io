VOD Clipping — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

VOD Clipping is a personal tool used only by BrimboJingus to post short clips of his own Twitch streams to his own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.

YouTube

VOD Clipping uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Data handled by Google is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

TikTok and Instagram

Posting to these accounts uses the TikTok Content Posting API and the Instagram API. Their own policies apply to data they handle: TikTok Privacy Policy and Instagram Privacy Policy.

What the tool accesses

After BrimboJingus signs in, the tool can upload videos to his account, read his account or channel name (to confirm it posts to the right place), and delete test videos it uploaded itself. It does not read anyone else's data, messages, followers or comments.

What is stored, and where

The sign-in permissions and a list of which clips were posted (file name, date and the post's ID) are stored only on BrimboJingus's own computer. Nothing is sent anywhere except to the platform the clip is posted to. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be removed at any time: Google/YouTube at Google security settings; TikTok under Settings > Security > Manage app permissions; Instagram under Settings > Apps and websites. Removing access, or deleting the saved sign-in files from the computer, removes all stored data. Saved sign-ins are deleted within 7 days of access being removed.
