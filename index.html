Funny moments from the stream, turned into Shorts

VOD Clipping is the tool BrimboJingus uses to turn his own Twitch stream recordings into short vertical clips, and to post them to his own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts.

Watch BrimboJingus live on Twitch

How it works

Finds the moments

After a stream, the tool reads the recording's speech, loud reactions, Twitch chat and viewers' own clips to find the funniest moments.

Builds a vertical clip

Each moment becomes a 1080×1920 video: the facecam on top, the game below, and bold word-by-word captions.

BrimboJingus reviews

Nothing goes out on its own. He watches every clip, keeps the good ones, asks for edits and approves which ones get posted.

Posts once a day

Approved clips are posted one per day to his own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts, with a link back to the Twitch channel.

Posting to TikTok

VOD Clipping uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API. BrimboJingus signs in with his TikTok account once; the tool then uploads an approved clip to his account with the caption "Follow BrimboJingus on Twitch (link in bio)" and hashtags. It only posts to his own account and does not read anyone else's data.

Posting to YouTube

The finished clips are posted as YouTube Shorts on the BrimboJingus YouTube channel.

VOD Clipping uses YouTube API Services to upload to that channel. See the Privacy Policy for how YouTube data is handled, and the YouTube Terms of Service.

FAQ

Who can use VOD Clipping?

Only BrimboJingus. It is a personal tool for his own channel and is not offered to the public, so there is no sign-up.

Does it post without anyone checking?

No. Every clip is watched and approved by BrimboJingus before it can be posted.

What data does it keep?

Only the sign-in permissions for his own accounts and a list of what was posted, stored on his own computer. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
